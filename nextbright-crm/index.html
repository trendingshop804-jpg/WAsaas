WhatsApp Channels

WhatsApp Channels is a one-way broadcast feature available directly inside the WhatsApp app. It is a separate product from the WhatsApp Business Platform (API). Channel creation, management and publishing are done through the WhatsApp app itself, not through the Business API your CRM uses.

⚠️
Important API Limitation: The WhatsApp Business Platform API used by this CRM does not support WhatsApp Channel creation or publishing. Your WABA (WhatsApp Business Account) credentials control messaging to individual contacts using approved templates — they do not grant Channel access.
What IS supported via this CRM:
  • ✅ Sending approved template messages to individual contacts
  • ✅ WhatsApp Campaigns to segmented audiences
  • ✅ Two-way messaging within the 24-hour service window
  • ✅ Delivery, read and reply status tracking
To use WhatsApp Channels:
  • Open the WhatsApp app on your phone
  • Go to Updates → + (plus icon)
  • Select "New channel" and follow the setup wizard
